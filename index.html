Loading face recognition…
  • Stand facing a window or a light so it shines on your face. Do not stand with the sun or a bright window behind you.
  • Avoid strong direct sunlight on the face; shade or indoor light is best.
  • Remove sunglasses, caps and face masks. Ordinary glasses are fine.
  • Hold the phone or stand at eye level, about an arm's length away, with your whole face inside the circle.
  • Look straight at the camera and keep still until the circle turns green.

Face photo

Look straight at the camera and press Capture photo. The app counts down 3, 2, 1, then takes the photo.
